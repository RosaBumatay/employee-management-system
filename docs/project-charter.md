## 1. Project Overview

The Wrio Byte Employee Management System (EMS) is a proposed web-based application designed to help organizations manage employee information and selected HR processes in one centralized system.

The initial release will focus on employee profile management, department and position management, attendance monitoring, leave requests and approvals, and basic reporting.

This project will be developed as a portfolio project to demonstrate project planning, requirements management, Agile/Scrum coordination, software development collaboration, and quality assurance.

## 2. Business Problem and Justification

Organizations may rely on spreadsheets, paper records, or disconnected tools to manage employee information and attendance. These approaches can make it difficult to maintain consistent records, monitor employee requests, and generate timely reports.

The proposed EMS aims to provide a centralized and organized solution that can:

- Improve employee record management.
- Reduce repetitive administrative work.
- Make attendance and leave information easier to monitor.
- Provide role-based access to relevant information.
- Support basic HR reporting and decision-making.

## 3. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Develop a functional web-based Employee Management System.
2. Implement secure login and role-based access for administrators, HR staff, and employees.
3. Provide employee profile creation, viewing, updating, and deletion.
4. Support department and position management.
5. Provide attendance monitoring.
6. Allow employees to submit leave requests and authorized HR users to review them.
7. Provide a dashboard and basic reports.
8. Complete testing, documentation, and a portfolio demonstration.

## 4. Project Scope

### 4.1 In Scope

The initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will include:

- User authentication and logout.
- Role-based access control.
- Administrator, HR Staff, and Employee roles.
- Employee profile management (CRUD).
- Department and position management.
- Attendance recording and monitoring.
- Leave application and approval workflow.
- Dashboard with basic employee and attendance summaries.
- Basic reports and data export.
- Testing and technical documentation.
- Deployment or a demonstrable local development setup.

### 4.2 Out of Scope

The following features are excluded from the initial release:

- Payroll processing.
- Biometric attendance hardware integration.
- Native Android or iOS applications.
- Government contribution and tax calculations.
- Accounting software integrations.
- Advanced predictive analytics.
- Complex enterprise resource planning features.

These may be considered for future versions.

## 8. Proposed Agile/Scrum Approach

The project will use an Agile/Scrum-inspired approach.

The project team will:

- Maintain a prioritized Product Backlog.
- Plan development work in short sprints.
- Conduct regular progress reviews.
- Track tasks using Trello.
- Review completed features against acceptance criteria.
- Record issues and improvements.
- Adjust priorities based on feedback and technical findings.

The proposed sprint duration is two weeks, subject to team agreement.

## 10. Initial Risks and Assumptions

### Key Risks

- Development team members may not be available when needed.
- The technology stack may require adjustment after feasibility review.
- Requirements may change during development.
- Integration issues may affect the schedule.
- Testing may identify defects requiring additional work.

### Assumptions

- The initial MVP will remain limited to the agreed scope.
- Synthetic employee data will be used for demonstrations and testing.
- Developers will participate in technical planning and estimation.
- Stakeholders will provide feedback within agreed review periods.
- The project timeline will be adjusted if resources or technical constraints require it.

## 11. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

1. The agreed MVP features are implemented and demonstrated.
2. Users can access features according to their assigned roles.
3. Employee records can be managed successfully.
4. Attendance and leave workflows function according to approved requirements.
5. Basic dashboard and reporting features are available.
6. Critical defects are resolved or documented with an agreed disposition.
7. Project and technical documentation are available.
8. The final system can be demonstrated using synthetic data.
