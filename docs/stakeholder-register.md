## 1. Purpose

This stakeholder register identifies individuals and roles that may influence, contribute to, or be affected by the Wrio Byte Employee Management System project.

It defines their expected responsibilities, level of influence, level of interest, and proposed communication approach.

Because this is a portfolio project, some stakeholders are proposed or simulated roles. They should not be represented as actual participants until formally assigned.

## 3. Stakeholder Roles and Responsibilities

### Project Owner / Sponsor
- Provides overall project direction.
- Reviews major scope or schedule changes.
- Supports key project decisions.
- Reviews the final project outcome.

### Project Manager
- Maintains the project plan and documentation.
- Coordinates stakeholder communication.
- Tracks tasks, milestones, and project risks.
- Facilitates planning and progress discussions.
- Reports project status and escalates blockers.

### Product Owner
- Maintains and prioritizes the Product Backlog.
- Clarifies business requirements.
- Defines expected feature outcomes.
- Reviews completed features against acceptance criteria.

### Scrum Master
- Helps the team follow agreed Scrum practices.
- Facilitates Scrum events when assigned.
- Helps identify and resolve impediments.
- Encourages collaboration and continuous improvement.

### HR Manager / Representative
- Explains HR processes and business needs.
- Reviews employee management workflows.
- Validates attendance and leave requirements.
- Provides feedback on system functionality.

### Development Team
- Evaluates technical feasibility.
- Estimates and implements development tasks.
- Participates in sprint planning and reviews.
- Documents technical decisions.
- Resolves development issues.

### QA Tester
- Prepares test scenarios and test cases.
- Tests functionality and role-based permissions.
- Documents defects and reproduction steps.
- Retests resolved issues.

### Employees
- Represent end-user needs.
- Provide feedback on usability.
- Use employee-facing features such as profile viewing and leave applications.

| Employees / End Users | Demonstrations and feedback sessions | At review milestones | Usability feedback |

## 5. Stakeholder Engagement Strategy

The project will use the following engagement approaches:

- **Manage closely:** Project Owner, Project Manager, Product Owner, and HR Representative.
- **Keep satisfied:** Stakeholders with significant decision-making or technical influence.
- **Keep informed:** Developers, QA Tester, HR Staff, and Employees.
- **Monitor:** Stakeholders with limited involvement who may become relevant later.

Engagement levels will be reviewed as the project progresses.

## 6. Stakeholder Management Considerations

- Confirm stakeholder availability before assigning responsibilities.
- Record major decisions and feedback.
- Clarify who has authority to approve requirements and scope changes.
- Keep communication consistent and documented.
- Update this register when team members or stakeholder responsibilities change.
