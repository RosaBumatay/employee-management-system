## 1. Purpose

This document provides a template for planning and recording the development sprints of the Employee Management System.

Each sprint should have a defined goal, selected Product Backlog Items, agreed responsibilities, and clear acceptance criteria.

## 2. Proposed Sprint Schedule

| Sprint | Proposed Period | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Weeks 3–4 | Core employee management |
| Sprint 2 | Weeks 5–6 | Attendance and leave management |
| Sprint 3 | Week 7 | Dashboard, reports, and refinements |
| Final Project Phase | Week 8 | Testing, deployment, documentation, and presentation |

The schedule is an initial estimate and requires team validation.

## 4. Sprint Review

At the end of each sprint, the team should:

1. Demonstrate completed functionality.
2. Review acceptance criteria.
3. Collect stakeholder feedback.
4. Record accepted and incomplete items.
5. Identify changes to the Product Backlog.

## 5. Sprint Retrospective

The team should discuss:

- What went well?
- What could be improved?
- What challenges occurred?
- What actions should be taken in the next sprint?
