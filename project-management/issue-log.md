## 1. Purpose

This Issue Log records problems, blockers, defects, and other events that have already occurred and may affect the Employee Management System project.

It helps the project team assign ownership, monitor resolution progress, and maintain visibility of issues that could affect project scope, schedule, quality, or delivery.

## 2. Issue Tracking Guidelines

Each issue should have:

- A unique issue ID.
- A clear description of the problem.
- The date it was identified.
- Its impact and priority.
- An assigned owner.
- A proposed resolution or next action.
- A current status and resolution date, when applicable.

Issues should be reviewed during project check-ins and escalated according to the Communication Management Plan.

## 4. Priority Definitions

| Priority | Description |
|---|---|
| Critical | Requires immediate attention because project delivery, security, or essential operations may be seriously affected |
| High | Significant impact on project progress or deliverables |
| Medium | Needs resolution but can be managed within normal project coordination |
| Low | Minor issue with limited impact |

## 5. Issue Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Issue has been identified but not yet resolved |
| In Progress | A resolution is being worked on |
| Blocked | Resolution cannot proceed because of a dependency or obstacle |
| Resolved | Corrective action has been completed |
| Closed | Resolution has been verified and the issue is formally closed |

## 6. Issue Escalation

Issues should be escalated when:

- They remain unresolved and threaten sprint goals.
- They affect important project milestones.
- They require a decision or approval beyond the assigned owner's authority.
- They introduce significant security, data, or operational concerns.

Escalation should follow the Communication Management Plan.

## 7. Review and Maintenance

The Project Coordinator will maintain this log and coordinate regular reviews with the relevant team members.

Issue owners are responsible for providing updates on their assigned issues.

Resolved issues should include a short explanation of the action taken and the date resolved.
